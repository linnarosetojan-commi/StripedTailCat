About

With a background in biological sciences, I am interested in bio-inspired design and biomechanics focusing on how biological principles can solve complex mechanical and structural challenges. My drive is to study nature's physical principles and translate them into great, functional engineering designs for real world problems using CAD modeling, structural analysis, and kinetic prototyping.

Skills

  • CAD modelling
  • Structural Simulation and Analysis- Finite element analysis, Multi Body Dynamics, Vibrational analysis
  • Biomechanical Analysis - 3D Anatomical reconstruction, Muscle Simulation, Inverse Kinematics, Force analysis
  • 3D Printing
  • Programming - Python, JAVA
  • Tools - COMSOL, Ansys, ArtiSynth, Blender, Onshape, Dragonfly
